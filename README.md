# Avocado Price Prediction & Market Analysis
Predictive analysis of avocado consumption patterns and pricing trends across different cities in the USA using multilinear regression.

## 1. Business Problem
* **1.1 Business Objective**: Analyze consumption patterns of avocados across various US cities and build a robust multilinear regression prediction model for pricing.
* **1.2 Constraints**: Address multicollinearity among regional volume and seasonal features.

## 2. Data Pre-processing & EDA
* Performed rigorous data cleaning, encoding categorical region data, and standardizing numerical features.
* Conducted univariate and bivariate analysis to observe seasonal and geographical pricing trends.

## 3. Model Building & Evaluation
* Trained multilinear regression models on scaled feature subsets.
* Validated assumptions using VIF scores, diagnostic plots, and residual analysis.
* Tabulated training vs. testing **RMSE** and **R-Squared** metrics.

## 4. Business Impact
* Assists freelance market analysts and suppliers in forecasting price fluctuations and optimizing supply chain distribution across cities
